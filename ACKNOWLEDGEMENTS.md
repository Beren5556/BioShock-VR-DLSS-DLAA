# Acknowledgements

## Mohamad Balouza · creator of BioShock VR

This fork is possible only because of the work of
**[Mohamad Balouza](https://github.com/mohamad-balouza)**, creator of
[BioShock VR](https://github.com/VR-Stereo-Hub/bioshock-trilogy-vr).

Mohamad solved the fundamental and most difficult part: integrating BioShock
with virtual reality, stereoscopic rendering, 6DOF head tracking, motion
controllers, hands, aiming, HUD and compatibility with different VR runtimes.
This repository builds on that foundation to add DLSS/DLAA and simplify
installation. It does not seek to replace the original project or claim its work.

Our sincere thanks go to Mohamad and everyone contributing to
**VR-Stereo-Hub**. Please visit, follow and support the original project:

- [Original BioShock VR repository](https://github.com/VR-Stereo-Hub/bioshock-trilogy-vr)
- [Exact upstream base v0.8.2](https://github.com/VR-Stereo-Hub/bioshock-trilogy-vr/releases/tag/v0.8.2)
- [Mohamad Balouza's profile](https://github.com/mohamad-balouza)
